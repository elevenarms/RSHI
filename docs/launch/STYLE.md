# SparkleTeam launch: art direction

One look for the paper, deck, website, video and Studio. The register is a pioneering research
release: calm, exact, confident, with a dry wink that the subject (dishes) earns. Think a lab
announcing a result it can defend, not a startup selling one.

## Voice

- Declarative and precise. Numbers carry the drama: "+17.8 points on four drills it had never seen."
- Gravitas first, wit second. The joke is always the deadpan contrast between rigorous method and
  dirty bowls; never slapstick, never emoji, never exclamation marks.
- Honest by default: every headline number sits next to its n, its uncertainty or its caveat.
- Credit generously: DishwasherBench is Composo's; models are NVIDIA Nemotron; inference is Crusoe.
- Names: SPARKLETEAM (caps in banners and titles, SparkleTeam in running text). The paper is
  "SparkleTeam: Towards Recursive Self Home Improvement".

## Type

- Monospace dominates: JetBrains Mono (fallback IBM Plex Mono). Weights 400 / 600 / 800.
- One serif for titles only, if a surface needs the classic-paper feel (Source Serif / Times-like).
- Big numbers in heavy monospace or tight grotesk; labels in small uppercase monospace with
  letter-spacing (0.08em).

## Colour (meaning only)

| Token | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ink | #000000 | text, rules, tags, UI chrome |
| paper | #FFFFFF | background (off-white #F6F6F4 for 3D plates) |
| clean / accepted / gain | #0A9D58 | |
| dirty / rejected / loss | #D64545 | |
| held-out exam / caution | #E6A100 | |
| grey (reasoning, secondary) | #8A8A8A | |

Role accents only on that role's marker: Planner blue, Loader cyan, Critic pink, Coach violet,
Librarian green-teal, Gatekeeper black. Nothing else gets colour.

## Layout

- White space is a feature. Black hairline rules (1px), black-filled tags for section labels
  ("FINDING", "METHOD", "EXAM").
- A subtle top banner on every surface: `SPARKLETEAM // DISHWASHING SCIENCE DIVISION`.
- Figures are framed like a journal: a numbered caption under every image ("Fig. 3 · …").
- 3D benchmark imagery (the Bosch machine, items with visible soil, water spray) on light
  neutral plates; never on dark backgrounds.

## Motion (video, Studio)

- Slow, deliberate, eased (ease-in-out, 0.6–1.2 s). Lines draw on; numbers count up; nothing
  bounces. Packets travel along hairline edges.
- Cuts land on the voice-over's full stops. Music: sparse, low, piano or soft synth pad.

## Signature moments

1. The exam reveal: "+17.8 pp" in green, huge, then the footnote "n = 4 · p ≈ 0.31 · a demonstration".
2. Finding 1 (bowl orientation): a deadpan headline with the measured effect, then the table.
3. The genome diff: three green lines of playbook, typed like code.
